# Apurva & Srishti | Wedding Invitation

A luxury Indian royal wedding invitation website for **Aurva & Srishti** (20 – 25 November 2026, Jaipur, Rajasthan).

## ✨ Features

- **Animated Video Curtain**: Fullscreen curtain reveal with sound and smooth transition.
- **Ambient Background Music**: Integrated romantic soundtrack with floating play/pause control.
- **Falling Petal Particles**: Natural flower petals scatter gracefully upon tapping the hero area.
- **Live 3D Flip Countdown Timer**: Real-time animated flip cards counting down to the wedding vows.
- **Interactive Sky Lanterns**: "Release lanterns" button sends floating lanterns into the sky with multi-layer depth.
- **Complete Auspicious Celebrations Timeline**: Detailed timeline for Peele Chawal, Sankdi Bindayak, Mehndi, Haldi, Mayra, Musical Night, Nikaasi, Baraat, and Panigrahan Sanskar.
- **Family Blessings & Invocation**: Traditional invocations including Shree Ganeshaya Namah, parents & grandparents blessings, and special requests.
- **RSVP & Contact**: Direct phone dial links for RSVP coordinators and host contact.
- **1-Click Calendar Add**: Automatic `.ics` (iCalendar) generation containing all 9 ceremonies.
- **Google Maps Navigation**: Direct link to Hotel Clarks Amer and Residence in Jaipur.
- **Mobile-First & Fully Responsive**: Designed for all phone, tablet, and desktop viewports.

## 🚀 Running Locally

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser or serve locally:

```bash
npx serve -l 3000 .
```
